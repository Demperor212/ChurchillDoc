Option Classes

Always remember option classes are optional!

(I mean, thats crazy right, option classes are optional)

But! Sometimes you forget about that, you take 67 options a year and fill all your spares, you panik because you don't have time to do academics anymore because you have all those option classes to do, you can't cope, you fail

Options are for FUN! If you are not having fun in option, you should consider not taking it and just take a spares

If options conflict with academic courses, always prioritize academic courses. Universities do not care if you got a 50 in an option, but they are going to care so much if you get a 50 in math

Options teaches you invaluable skills you otherwise would not have learned, like in Design studies you learn how to CAD, in Mechanics you learn how to fix a car, in food you learn food, etc. Those skills should be the ones you already want to learn: Remember options are optional!

Check your school's website for option classes they offer.

There are new Digital future options, according a friend of mine who took Engineering intro, they are fun, you 3d print, lazer cut, stuff, etc.

It is good if you learn some skills relavent to the field you want to get into, but universities generally don't care about options. It is always academics you are prioritizing because thats what universities care about when they decide whether to choose you or not! Those skills you build are helpful later in your path, like when you are in university and you don't have to start coding from scratch / you already know a bit about Autodesk software...

You can drop options if you feel like they are not what you want to learn.

As always, have fun in options!
